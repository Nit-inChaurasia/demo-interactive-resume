A Note

On the way here

a fairly non-linear path, told honestly

I never set out to have a tidy career.

I studied mechanical engineering at NIT Karnataka (top 0.8% of over 9.4 lakh JEE Mains candidates), and somewhere along the way decided that building things mattered more to me than designing them on paper.

While still in college, I kept finding my way into rooms where something needed fixing: consulting projects for clients across the US, Nigeria and India with 180 Degrees Consulting, a flagship E-Cell event for 1,000+ attendees, a tech fest that needed sponsors and didn't have any until I went and raised a 7-figure number for it.

Then I went looking for where decisions about the future actually get made, and ended up on the other side of the table, selected as a VC Fellow from over 1,500 global applicants, and later as a Resident at a Silicon Valley-based early-stage fund, screening startup deals and learning what actually makes an LP say yes.

Today I'm part of the founding team at DAZZL, where most of my job is turning zero into something: zero professionals into a hundred-plus, zero hubs into five, zero users into tens of thousands, in under a year.

What ties it together isn't a title. It's curiosity, mostly, and a fairly stubborn habit of asking why one too many times. If you're building, investing, or just like talking about startups, markets or strategy, we'll probably get along.
